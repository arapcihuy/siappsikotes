The Problem

When you share a link on Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, or WhatsApp, the preview image (OG image) sometimes doesn't appear. This happens because:

  • The og:image tag is missing or incorrect
  • The image URL is blocked or inaccessible
  • The image doesn't meet the platform's size or format requirements
  • The platform's cache hasn't been updated
